1. Brazilian E-Commerce Analysis — Olist

End-to-end analysis of a Brazilian e-commerce marketplace, using SQL, PostgreSQL and Power BI to investigate sales performance, delivery efficiency and customer satisfaction.

GitHub Repository Sales Dashboard (PDF) Logistics & Satisfaction (PDF) Power BI File

Business Objective

Evaluate marketplace sales trends and identify how delivery performance relates to customer satisfaction, generating actionable insights for business decisions.

Key Findings

  • Sales increased by approximately 138% when comparing January–August 2017 with the same period in 2018.
  • Orders delivered on time received an average review score of 4.29, compared with 2.57 for late deliveries.
  • Negative reviews represented 9.19% of on-time deliveries and 53.99% of late deliveries.

Business Recommendations

  • Monitor delivery delays by seller, carrier and location.
  • Prioritize operational improvements for orders at risk of late delivery.
  • Track sales trends and customer satisfaction through business-focused dashboards.

Tools: SQL, PostgreSQL, Power BI, data validation, KPI analysis and business reporting.

Objetivo de negocio

Evaluar las tendencias de ventas del marketplace e identificar cómo el desempeño de las entregas se relaciona con la satisfacción del cliente, generando información útil para la toma de decisiones.

Principales hallazgos

  • Las ventas aumentaron aproximadamente un 138% al comparar enero–agosto de 2017 con el mismo periodo de 2018.
  • Los pedidos entregados a tiempo obtuvieron una calificación promedio de 4,29, frente a 2,57 para las entregas tardías.
  • Las reseñas negativas representaron el 9,19% de las entregas a tiempo y el 53,99% de las entregas tardías.

Recomendaciones de negocio

  • Monitorear los retrasos por vendedor, transportista y ubicación.
  • Priorizar mejoras operativas en los pedidos con riesgo de entrega tardía.
  • Hacer seguimiento a las ventas y la satisfacción del cliente mediante dashboards orientados al negocio.

Herramientas: SQL, PostgreSQL, Power BI, validación de datos, análisis de indicadores e informes de negocio.

3. Telecom Contact Center Performance Analysis

Analysis of contact center operations to identify inefficient operators, missed interactions and opportunities to improve service performance.

GitHub Repository

Key Findings

  • Failed outbound calls represented 52.56% of the analyzed interactions, compared with 41.46% for inbound calls.
  • A statistical comparison showed a significant difference between these proportions (z = 21.433, p < 0.001).
  • Operator performance metrics can help prioritize coaching and operational follow-up.

Business Recommendations

  • Monitor operator-level KPIs and failed interactions.
  • Investigate the main causes of unsuccessful calls.
  • Use performance dashboards to guide coaching and resource allocation.

Tools: Python, Pandas, data visualization and hypothesis testing.
