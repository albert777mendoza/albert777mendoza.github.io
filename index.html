Olist E-commerce Analytics

SQL · PostgreSQL · Power BI

An end-to-end analysis of Brazilian e-commerce data to understand sales performance, delivery reliability, and customer satisfaction.

  • Sales growth: approximately 138% from January–August 2017 to the same period in 2018.
  • On-time deliveries: average review score of 4.29 versus 2.57 for late deliveries.
  • Negative reviews: 9.19% for on-time deliveries versus 53.99% for late deliveries.
GitHub Repository Sales Dashboard (PDF) Logistics & Satisfaction Dashboard (PDF) Power BI File

Business Questions

  • How did sales evolve over time?
  • How are delivery performance and customer satisfaction related?
  • Which logistics indicators can help prioritize improvements?

Approach

Used SQL and PostgreSQL to query and validate relational e-commerce data, then built Power BI dashboards to communicate commercial and logistics findings.

Business Takeaway

Delivery performance is strongly associated with customer satisfaction. Late deliveries deserve attention because they coincide with substantially lower ratings and a much higher share of negative reviews.

El desempeño de las entregas está fuertemente asociado con la satisfacción del cliente. Las entregas tardías merecen atención porque coinciden con calificaciones considerablemente menores y una mayor proporción de reseñas negativas.

Analysis Scope

  • Order delivery performance and delays.

The analysis supports prioritizing delivery reliability as a potential lever for improving customer experience. The observed differences are descriptive and do not, by themselves, prove causation.

Hotel Booking Analysis

Python · Pandas · Seaborn · Matplotlib · SQL · SQLAlchemy

Strategic analysis of 119,390 hotel bookings from 2015–2017 to identify cancellation patterns, revenue exposure, and opportunities to improve booking management.

  • Cancellation rate: 37% overall; 41.7% for City Hotel and 27.8% for Resort Hotel.
  • Revenue at risk: approximately €16.72 million in estimated lost revenue associated with cancellations.
  • Potential opportunity: reducing City Hotel cancellations from 41.7% to 30% could represent approximately €3.2 million in recoverable revenue under the project's assumptions.
GitHub Repository Jupyter Notebook Interactive Dashboard

Business Questions

  • Which hotel type experiences more cancellations?
  • How do booking lead time, customer type, and deposit policy relate to cancellation risk?
  • Which distribution channels generate the most revenue?
  • Where could hotel management prioritize cancellation-reduction efforts?

Key Findings

  • City Hotel had a higher cancellation rate than Resort Hotel.
  • Longer booking lead times and prior cancellation history were useful risk indicators.
  • Online Travel Agencies generated approximately €13.7 million in revenue in the analyzed data.
  • City Hotel accounted for about 65.1% of the estimated cancellation-related lost revenue.
  • Average Daily Rate (ADR) reached a peak of approximately €125.17 in the analyzed period.

Methodology

Cleaned and explored booking data using Python and Pandas, analyzed cancellation and revenue patterns, and used visualizations to compare hotel types, booking characteristics, and distribution channels.

Recommendations

  • Prioritize cancellation-risk monitoring for City Hotel bookings.
  • Review booking policies for reservations with long lead times and a history of cancellations.
  • Track cancellation rates, ADR, and revenue exposure by hotel type and channel.

Note: the €3.2 million figure is an estimated opportunity based on the project's assumptions, not guaranteed recovered revenue.

Contact Center Operational Efficiency Analysis

Python · Pandas · Statistical Analysis · Tableau

Analysis of contact center activity to identify missed-call patterns, operational risks, and opportunities to improve customer access and operator performance.

  • Outbound failed-call rate: 52.56%, compared with 41.46% for inbound calls.
  • Statistical test: Z = 21.433, p < 0.001, indicating a statistically significant difference in the analyzed sample.
  • Estimated opportunity: the project estimated approximately US$6,000 per month in potential productivity loss.
Project README Jupyter Notebook Tableau Dashboard

Business Questions

  • Do inbound and outbound calls show different failed-call rates?
  • Which operators may need performance support or further investigation?
  • How can missed calls inform operational improvement priorities?

Key Findings

  • Outbound calls had a higher failed-call rate than inbound calls in the analyzed data.
  • The difference was statistically significant (Z = 21.433, p < 0.001).
  • The project found a moderate positive correlation (r = 0.52) between missed calls and its operational-performance measure.
  • Operator segmentation identified groups requiring closer monitoring and potential coaching.

Methodology

Prepared and analyzed call-level data with Python and Pandas, examined operational indicators, compared inbound and outbound outcomes, and applied statistical testing to assess the observed difference.

Recommendations

  • Investigate the causes of unsuccessful outbound calls.
  • Monitor missed-call rates and operator-level indicators over time.
  • Use targeted coaching and staffing reviews to address recurring operational issues.

The financial estimate is an analytical estimate from the project, rather than a verified accounting loss.

A/B Testing — E-commerce Recommendation System

Python · Pandas · SciPy · Statistical Hypothesis Testing

Evaluation of an e-commerce recommendation-system experiment to determine whether the observed conversion results provided sufficient evidence to support implementation.

  • p-value: 0.3024.
  • Decision: no statistically significant improvement was detected at the 5% significance level.
  • Business implication: the available evidence was insufficient to justify rollout based on conversion improvement.
Project README Jupyter Notebook

Business Questions

  • Did the new recommendation experience improve conversion?
  • Was the observed difference statistically significant?
  • Were there experiment-design issues that could affect the conclusion?

Key Findings

  • The hypothesis test returned a p-value of 0.3024, above the 0.05 significance threshold.
  • The observed funnel difference was approximately -1.1 percentage points in the analyzed comparison.
  • Potential group imbalance, user contamination, and temporal effects were identified as considerations for interpretation.

Methodology

Prepared the experiment data, reviewed the conversion funnel, formulated hypotheses, and used statistical testing to evaluate the evidence for a difference between groups.

Recommendation

Do not claim a positive impact or implement the change solely on the basis of these results. Review assignment quality, sample balance, experiment duration, and possible contamination before running a better-controlled test.

Book Database Analysis

SQL · Relational Data Analysis · Python

Analysis of a relational book database to explore publishing activity, user engagement, ratings, reviews, and author performance.

  • Examined publishers and the concentration of books across publishing catalogs.
  • Compared ratings with written reviews to understand different forms of user engagement.
  • Explored differences in author output, ratings, and review activity.
Project README Jupyter Notebook

Business Questions

  • Which publishers have the largest book catalogs?
  • How do ratings and written reviews differ as measures of engagement?
  • Which authors show stronger output or user response?
  • How can relational queries turn database records into business insights?

Key Findings

  • Publishing activity was concentrated among publishers such as Penguin Books and Vintage.
  • Ratings were more common than written reviews.
  • Highly rated books were not necessarily the books with the most reviews.
  • Users who rated more than 50 books wrote an average of approximately 24 reviews in the analyzed dataset.
  • Authors differed in publication volume, ratings, and review activity.

Methodology

Queried relational tables with SQL to aggregate book, publisher, author, rating, and review data, then interpreted the results in a business context.

Business Takeaway

Ratings and written reviews capture different types of engagement. Combining both measures provides a more useful view of reader activity than relying on a single metric.
